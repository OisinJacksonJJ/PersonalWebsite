01 / People, practice, perspective

Engagement & learning

Learning does not happen in isolation. Here I reflect on the people, experiences, and professional communities that help me grow.

Learning from others

Ideas are better when shared.

Over the past few years partaken in talks and seminars with industry professionals, covering a wide range of topics such as the integration between karst groundwater and modelling, to building and scaling technology companies in Europe

A more personal, smaller example was the Burren Geopark Academy in May 2026, which celebrated the work of Dr David Drew and highlighted ongoing research in the area, including my own.

The key piece of knowledge I learned from this event was that no matter how isolated or small an aspect of the world around us is, the scientific method and human curiosity can lead you down a path of discovery to understand the how and why behind it all

Additionally, I was surprised by how increasingly integrated software is becoming with the physical landscape. Much of the newly published research presented that day was integrated with software, such as the mapping of submarine groundwater discharges off the Burren coast

Engaging with the professional industry

Staying connected to the wider picture.

I have looked for opportunities to learn from people working in technology and related fields

One of the recent highlights being the International AI Summit, which took place in Dublin in October 2026. During this event I got to discuss and learn about the technological landscape by talking to representatives from companies such as:

-Stripe, who invited me as a delegate to represent the Stripe Young Scientist.

-OpenAI, where I discussed how one of the forefront companies of the AI revolution is working to implement it's technology into other sectors of the workforce

-Bentley, which showed how to cross the bridge between infastructure and city planning and software

-Intel, where I got a sneak peak into upcoming processor models and what goes into making the world's cutting edge hardware technology

Putting learning into practice

From insight to action.

These learning opportunities and experiences gave many new perspectives about how I engage with technology, from the little things such as my AI usage to how I want to present myself to the professional world
