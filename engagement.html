01 / People, practice, perspective

Engagement & learning

Learning does not happen in isolation. Here I reflect on the people, experiences, and professional communities that help me grow.

Learning from others

Ideas are better when shared.

Over the past few years I have partaken in talks and seminars with industry professionals, covering a wide range of topics such as the integration between karst groundwater and modelling, to building and scaling technology companies in Europe

A more personal, smaller example was the Burren Geopark Academy in May 2026, which celebrated the work of Dr David Drew and highlighted ongoing research in the area, including my own.

The key piece of knowledge I learned from this event was that no matter how isolated or small an aspect of the world around us is, the scientific method and human curiosity can lead you down a path of discovery to understand the how and why behind it all

Additionally, I was surprised by how increasingly integrated software is becoming with the physical landscape. Much of the newly published research presented that day was integrated with software, such as the mapping of submarine groundwater discharges off the Burren coast

Engaging with the professional industry

Staying connected to the wider picture.

I have looked for opportunities to learn from people working in technology and related fields.

Dublin / October 2026

International AI Summit

At the summit, I spoke with company representatives and learned about different parts of the technology landscape.

  • Stripe Invited me as a delegate representing Stripe Young Scientist.
  • OpenAI Discussed how AI technologies are being introduced into other sectors of the workforce.
  • Bentley Explored how software connects with infrastructure and city planning.
  • Intel Got a preview of upcoming processor models and learned about the work behind advanced hardware.

Putting learning into practice

From insight to action.

These opportunities gave me new perspectives on how I engage with technology, from my everyday use of AI to how I present myself professionally.

The International AI Summit helped me understand more about the technology landscape and how to begin building a professional presence. Since then, I have:

  • Created a LinkedIn profile.
  • Started considering which companies I might pursue through the ISE residency programme.
  • Begun looking at how everyday problems could inspire useful solutions or businesses.
